Math Exam Generator & Corrector

Generate & correct math exams automatically

Pick a grade and question count, and get an OMR-ready bubble sheet built from the school's math-area weighting table — then grade photographed answer sheets instantly, with a per-topic breakdown. No login required.

What it does

Exam Generator Teacher enters school name, grade, and question count (max 25). Questions are drawn from a 240-question bank and apportioned by that grade's area weights.
Printable bubble sheets Downloads a teacher copy (answers marked + answer key) and a blank student copy, both formatted for optical scanning.
Exam Corrector Upload a photo of a completed sheet. The exact printed bubble layout is used to perspective-correct the photo and grade it — no manual bubbling required.
Android app Grade straight from a phone camera using the same server, with results matching the web app.

Scope

Covers Cycle 2 (General track: G5–G8) and Cycle 3 (Advanced + General tracks: G9–G12) per the school's curriculum weighting table. Cycle 1 and Applied/Elective tracks are out of scope.
